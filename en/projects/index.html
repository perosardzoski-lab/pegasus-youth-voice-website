COMPLETED YOUTH ACTIVITY · 30 OCTOBER 2026 · SKOPJE

Halloween Digital Escape Room Challenge

ESCAPERO in action. At Youth Centre Karposh, young people explored our interactive digital escape-room experience, known at the time as Spooky Escape Rooms and now called ESCAPERO.

Hosted by Youth Centre Karposh and organised by Pegasus English Language School in cooperation with Pegasus Youth Voice, the activity brought together six young participants from Skopje. Three youth-team members helped introduce the challenge, guide participants and support teamwork as they solved mysteries, practised English and developed digital and problem-solving skills.
