Your voice belongs here: What young people can do with Pegasus Youth Voice

A place to explore ideas, share experiences, build skills and take part in your community — whether you love writing, making videos, learning new things or simply have a story to tell.

23 November 2026 · Pegasus Youth Voice editorial team

A welcoming invitation to share ideas, tell stories, build skills, volunteer and help shape a youth-led community.

Bring your voice

You do not need to be an expert or have an impressive CV to contribute. Your questions, everyday experiences and opinions matter. Suggest a topic that affects young people in Tetovo, interview someone whose story deserves to be heard, or write about an issue you care about.

Create something worth sharing

Interested in journalism, photography, podcasts, video, creative writing or cultural heritage? We want this platform to become a place where young people can develop stories and creative work together, with support, constructive feedback and respect for one another.

Learn by doing

Workshops, conversations and collaborative projects can help young people practise communication, media and AI literacy, critical thinking, teamwork and leadership. The goal is not only to learn something new, but to use those skills in ways that matter.

Take part in your community

Young people can suggest volunteering ideas, environmental actions, cultural activities and opportunities for dialogue across communities and generations. Meaningful change can begin with one small, practical idea.

Everyone is welcome

We want participation to be inclusive, respectful and voluntary. You can start by sharing an idea, exploring opportunities to contribute or getting in touch. You do not need previous experience. Activities and opportunities will be announced when confirmed.

This blog starts with an invitation, not a finished story. What should we talk about first? What would you like to create, learn or change? We would love to hear from you.
