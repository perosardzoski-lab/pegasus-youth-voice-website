Suggest your story

Submit your short idea directly to the Pegasus Youth Voice editorial team. You won't need an email application. Ideas are reviewed privately and are never published automatically.

Your email application should open with your story idea prepared. Please review and send the message there. If nothing opens, use the direct email link.

If online submission is unavailable, email us directly: pegasusyouthvoice@gmail.com

Please do not include sensitive personal details or identifying information about minors. Submissions are reviewed for accuracy, attribution, consent and safeguarding before publication.
