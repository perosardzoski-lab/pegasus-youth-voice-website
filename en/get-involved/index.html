What can we create together?

From podcasts, youth media and cultural stories to digital literacy, volunteering, environmental action and international cooperation, there is room for your ideas.

Who can join?

Everyone aged 14 or older who shares our values is welcome to express interest. No previous experience is required. We welcome young people, mentors and supporters from different backgrounds.

How membership works

Express your interest through our trilingual questionnaire. We will then explain the membership application and next steps. Submitting the questionnaire is not automatic membership; admission follows the association's Statute and a decision by the competent body. Applicants aged 14–17 need written consent from a legal guardian.
