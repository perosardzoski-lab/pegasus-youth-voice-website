PEGASUS YOUTH VOICE

Get involved

Whether you are a young person, educator, volunteer, artist, researcher or potential partner, we welcome ideas for youth-led learning, storytelling, culture and community action.

Ways to participate

Young people may eventually take part as contributors, volunteers, event participants or project collaborators. Educators, cultural workers and partner organisations may propose activities and cooperation.

Participation arrangements and open calls will be announced publicly when available.

Safe, inclusive participation

Our activities are guided by inclusion, non-discrimination, consent and respect for privacy. Before enabling online youth submissions, we will publish participation and safeguarding guidance.
