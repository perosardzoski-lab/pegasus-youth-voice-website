About Pegasus Youth Voice

Pegasus Youth Voice (ПЕГАСУС МЛАДИНСКИ ГЛАС) is a voluntary, non-governmental, non-partisan, non-profit and independent association based in Tetovo, RN Macedonia.

Our mission is grounded in youth participation, education, artistic and cultural expression, democratic engagement, social inclusion, sustainability and international cooperation.

Our mission

To encourage active, responsible, creative and critically minded young people through education, expression, intercultural dialogue and meaningful civic participation.

Our vision

Communities in which diverse young voices are heard, valued and equipped to contribute to positive change.

Our guiding principles

Voluntary participation, transparency, accountability, equal opportunities, non-discrimination, gender equality, respect for human rights, cultural diversity, intergenerational solidarity, environmental responsibility and ethical use of technology.

How we work

Our founding documents allow us to develop training, workshops, youth clubs, research, publications, cultural programmes, digital platforms and community initiatives. Each activity will be announced when its details are confirmed.

Governance and accountability

The association’s governance structure is defined by its Statute, including the Assembly, President/legal representative and Management Board. For questions about the association’s governance or approved public documents, contact us.
