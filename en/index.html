WELCOME TO PEGASUS YOUTH VOICE

Rooted in Tetovo. Open to the world.

Pegasus Youth Voice is an independent, non-governmental, non-partisan and non-profit association based in Tetovo, North Macedonia. We connect youth participation with learning, creativity, culture, digital literacy and civic engagement.

We believe young people deserve opportunities to explore, create, speak up, collaborate and help shape the communities around them.

Our mission

To encourage active, responsible, creative and critically minded young people through education, expression, intercultural dialogue and meaningful civic participation.

Our vision

Communities in which diverse young voices are heard, valued and equipped to contribute to positive change.

OUR FOCUS

Our areas of work

Our priorities are grounded in the association’s Statute and Programme of Action. These describe the directions of our work, not claims about completed projects.

01

Youth participation and civic action

Youth initiatives, volunteering, democratic participation and community dialogue.

02

Education and life skills

Non-formal learning, languages, communication, leadership and employability.

03

Digital, media and AI literacy

Critical thinking, online safety, responsible technology and media awareness.

04

Culture, creativity and heritage

Local stories, cultural memory, creative expression and intergenerational exchange.

05

Inclusion and wellbeing

Equal opportunities, social cohesion, safe participation and youth wellbeing.

06

Sustainability and local development

Climate awareness, greener practices, entrepreneurship and local initiatives.
